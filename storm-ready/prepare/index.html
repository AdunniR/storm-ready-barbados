StormReady Barbados

Get ready for hurricane season

Hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November 2026. Get ready before the season starts.

Household checklist

Use this checklist to make sure your household is ready. It covers water, food, documents, first aid, communication, and what to do before the storm hits.

Water

Store at least 3 gallons of drinking water per person for 3 days. Fill your bath and buckets before the storm hits.

Documents

Keep your national ID, insurance papers, and birth certificates in a waterproof bag. Have cash in small bills ready.

Communication

Charge all devices and a power bank. Have a battery radio to hear DEM updates. Write your emergency contacts on paper.

Before it hits

Board up windows, bring in outdoor items, top up your vehicle with petrol, and let family know your shelter plan.

Do I need to go to a shelter?

You do not always need to leave your home during a hurricane. It depends on where you live and what type of house you have.

You may be able to stay home if:

  • You live in a solid concrete house
  • You are away from the coast
  • You are not in a low-lying or flood-prone area
  • You have enough food, water, and supplies

Go to a Category 1 shelter if:

  • You live in a wooden house
  • You live near the coast or in a low-lying area
  • DEM tells you to evacuate
  • You do not feel safe at home

Only go to a shelter when DEM opens them. Category 1 shelters are only activated when DEM announces it. Wait for the official announcement before you travel. Find your nearest shelter →
