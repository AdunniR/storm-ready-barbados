StormReady Barbados

Get ready for hurricane season and stay up to date with Department of Emergency Management (DEM) storm alerts.

Hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November 2026. Get ready before the season starts — do not wait for a storm warning.

Household checklist

Use this checklist to make sure your household is ready. It covers water, food, documents, first aid, communication, and what to do before the storm hits.

The checklist opens in a new tab where you can tick items off. "Save as PDF" opens it and prompts your browser to save a PDF copy.

Water

Store at least 3 gallons of drinking water per person for 3 days. Fill your bath and buckets before the storm hits.

Documents

Keep your national ID, insurance papers, and birth certificates in a waterproof bag. Have cash in small bills ready.

Communication

Charge all devices and a power bank. Have a battery radio to hear DEM updates. Write your emergency contacts on paper.

Before it hits

Board up windows, bring in outdoor items, top up your vehicle with petrol, and let family know your shelter plan.
